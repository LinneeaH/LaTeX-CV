# Experience - Novo Nordisk

## Overview

Worked as a Junior Developer in a contract role at Novo Nordisk, contributing to the maintenance and continued development of an internal web application.

The role combined software development, testing, technical troubleshooting, monitoring, process improvement, and close collaboration within an agile development team. Alongside delivering technical changes, I contributed to making work clearer and more visible through improved backlog practices and regular team demonstrations.

This experience reinforced that effective software development depends not only on implementation quality, but also on shared context, clear priorities, feedback, and knowledge exchange.

## Professional Development

This role broadened my understanding of software development beyond implementation alone.

Working in a growing team demonstrated how task clarity, shared context, communication, and feedback influence both technical delivery and the ability of individuals to contribute effectively.

Initiating backlog clean-up sessions and helping establish regular demonstrations showed that useful improvements do not always require formal authority. Practical changes can emerge from noticing recurring friction, proposing a manageable response, and involving the relevant people.

The experience also reinforced my preference for development work connected to users, shared goals, and meaningful outcomes rather than isolated implementation without broader context.

---

## Lasting Insights

- Effective software development depends on shared understanding as well as technical quality.
- Clear and discoverable work makes it easier for team members to contribute independently.
- Regular demonstrations create useful feedback loops and strengthen appreciation across a team.
- Documentation and knowledge sharing support both onboarding and operational continuity.
- Lightweight process improvements can create meaningful value when they address genuine sources of friction.
- Technical solutions become more sustainable when responsibility and understanding are shared.
