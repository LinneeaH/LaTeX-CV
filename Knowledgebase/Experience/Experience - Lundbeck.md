# Experience – Lundbeck (Research Data Platform)

## Overview

Contributed to the development and continuous improvement of a cloud-based research data platform to support automated ingestion, processing, transformation, and reporting of scientific research data.

The role combined technical implementation, platform configuration, software development, deployment, troubleshooting, governance, and close collaboration with scientists, subject matter experts, vendors, and fellow developers. Rather than contributing only during implementation, the work frequently spanned the entire lifecycle of scientific use cases—from discovery and requirement clarification through solution design, implementation, validation, deployment, operational support, and continuous improvement.

Alongside delivering individual use cases, increasing attention was devoted to strengthening the platform itself by improving maintainability, reducing technical friction, increasing transparency, and establishing foundations that would support future growth.

### Improving Metadata Capture for Experimental Reproducibility

Identified that the data being captured for a scientific use case did not include all the contextual metadata required to reproduce the underlying experiments.

Took the initiative to investigate how the meta data capture could be improved to assure experiment reproducibility.  

This included:

- Reviewing the instrument software and available configuration options
- Determining which settings and metadata could be included in the exported data
- Collaborating with instrument vendors to clarify data-capture and export capabilities
- Investigating practical options for capturing the information required for reproducibility
- Documenting the relevant export configurations to support consistent instrument setup, knowledge transfer, and future onboarding

This work helped move the use case from identifying a limitation in the captured data towards a practical approach for preserving the scientific context required to understand and reproduce experiments.
