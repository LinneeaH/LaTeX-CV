# FAKTA

Fakta was a supermarked chain. 

## Role Overview

Worked initially as a Student Assistant in a Fakta store before being promoted to Closing Manager with responsibility for independently closing the store.

The role combined customer service, operational responsibility, and daily leadership within a fast-paced retail environment.

Compared with previous logistics roles, this position introduced direct customer interaction and responsibility for ensuring that an entire operation functioned successfully rather than contributing to only one part of it.

---

# Responsibilities

Daily responsibilities included:

- Customer service
- Cash register operation
- Stock replenishment
- Receiving deliveries
- Store presentation
- Cleaning and general maintenance
- Assisting colleagues wherever needed

Following promotion to Closing Manager, additional responsibilities included:

- Opening and closing procedures
- Responsibility for store keys
- Cash reconciliation
- Bank deposits
- Coordinating work during evening shifts
- Delegating tasks
- Supporting colleagues
- Ordering selected products
- Inventory management
- Ensuring store readiness for the following day
- Acting as the responsible employee in the manager's absence during closing shifts

---

# Leadership and Responsibility

The role represented the first experience with formal operational responsibility.

Although authority was part of the position, the most rewarding aspect was not managing people but helping colleagues succeed.

Frequently became the person colleagues approached with questions and practical problems.

Enjoyed:

- Supporting newer colleagues
- Helping customers
- Coordinating work when needed
- Creating calm during busy periods
- Taking responsibility for the overall operation

Leadership was experienced primarily as service to the team rather than supervision.

---

# Initiative and Ownership

The store manager created a culture with considerable trust and autonomy.

Employees were encouraged to think independently, suggest improvements, and take initiative rather than simply completing assigned tasks.

Examples included proposing practical ideas to improve sales and reduce food waste, such as finding better ways to sell bananas approaching the end of their shelf life instead of discarding them.

When daily responsibilities were completed before the end of a shift, Linnéa naturally looked for additional work rather than waiting for instructions.

Over time this developed into a recurring professional habit of actively improving the surrounding work rather than limiting effort to assigned tasks.

# Professional Development

The promotion to Closing Manager demonstrated confidence from management in Linnéa's judgement, reliability, and ability to handle responsibility independently.

Later, when changing jobs, the store manager personally recommended Linnéa to another FAKTA store.

Although the precise reasons were never explicitly discussed, the recommendation reflected a level of confidence developed through day-to-day collaboration and consistent performance.

This became another early example that strong professional relationships are built over time through dependable contribution rather than self-promotion.

---

# Personal Development

The role reinforced several preferences that have remained consistent throughout later education and employment.

Enjoyed:

- Feeling part of a team
- Building relationships with colleagues
- Helping people solve everyday problems
- Having independence within clear responsibilities
- Working in an environment built on mutual trust
- Being encouraged to contribute ideas
- Feeling that personal initiative made a visible difference

Linnéa also noticed a tendency to quickly identify with the teams and organisations she became part of, naturally referring to them as "our store" and caring deeply about shared success.

This strong sense of ownership continues to influence how she approaches collaboration today.

# Motivation

The most enjoyable aspects of the role were not the retail tasks themselves but the surrounding work environment.

Particularly motivating were:

- Freedom combined with responsibility
- Managers who trusted employees
- Encouragement to think independently
- Helping colleagues and customers
- Daily interaction with many different people
- Practical problem solving
- Seeing ideas implemented
- Feeling that individual contributions mattered

The role demonstrated that supportive leadership and a positive team culture can make relatively simple work highly engaging.

# Context

This role established several professional patterns that continued throughout later education and employment.

It reinforced that:

- Responsibility is motivating when accompanied by trust.
- Initiative is most rewarding when managers encourage it.

- Helping colleagues succeed is often more satisfying than directing them.
