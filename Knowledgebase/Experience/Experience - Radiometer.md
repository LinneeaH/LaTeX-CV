# Radiometer

## Role Overview

Worked as a Student Assistant, initially, in the Distribution department and later in Shipping following the relocation of the primary distribution department to Poland.

Although the work itself was relatively straightforward, the role provided early experience within a regulated healthcare company and reinforced several professional preferences that have remained consistent throughout later education and employment.

Unlike the large and highly dynamic logistics operation at BESTSELLER, Radiometer represented a considerably smaller and more controlled environment with greater emphasis on traceability, documentation, and consistency.

The work here added deepened understanding of course topics related to the medical device industry.  

---

# Responsibilities

### Distribution

Daily work included:

- Collecting customer orders
- Packing products for shipment
- Preparing outgoing deliveries
- Quality checking shipments
- Supporting day-to-day logistics operations
- Working with operational Excel spreadsheets

Certain products required packaging with dry ice.

Handling these shipments required additional training and careful adherence to established procedures due to the specialised packing requirements.

Although many colleagues preferred not to perform these tasks, Linnéa viewed them as a normal part of supporting the team and willingly helped wherever additional effort was needed.

### Shipping

When Radiometer relocated the primary distribution function to Poland, approximately four of thirteen student assistants were retained within the company.

Two transferred into departments related to their university studies.

Linnéa and one other colleague transferred into the Shipping department based on demonstrated performance and reliability.

Responsibilities included:

- Preparing invoices for customers
- Preparing credit notes
- Supporting shipping administration
- Archiving completed picking lists
- Supporting the administrative processes following completed shipments

- Healthcare logistics
- Traceability

# Personal Development

This role reinforced an approach to teamwork that has remained consistent throughout later roles.

Rather than optimising for preferred tasks, Linnéa naturally preferred helping where work was needed most.

Taking responsibility for less popular work, such as dry ice shipments, was viewed simply as part of contributing to the team and helping distribute workload fairly among colleagues.

The experience also reinforced that enjoyable work is created as much by colleagues, culture, and everyday interactions as by the technical complexity of the tasks themselves.

---

# Motivation

Although the work itself was repetitive and not intellectually demanding, the role remained highly enjoyable.

Particularly appreciated:

- Friendly colleagues
- Relaxed work environment
- Frequent interaction throughout the day
- Physical movement rather than prolonged desk work
- Being part of a supportive team
- Practical contribution to shared goals

This role reinforced an important personal observation that has continued throughout later career decisions:

Intellectual complexity alone is not a reliable predictor of job satisfaction.

Positive colleagues, a healthy work culture, trust, and a sense of belonging contribute at least as much to long-term enjoyment at work.

# Context

Radiometer represented an early introduction to the healthcare industry before later engineering roles within healthcare and life sciences.

Although the technical content of the role was limited, it contributed to a growing appreciation for regulated environments, structured processes, and collaborative workplaces.

Together with the earlier experience at BESTSELLER, the role also established several professional patterns that have remained consistent throughout later education and employment:

- Reliable performance leads to trust.
- Good workplace culture has a substantial impact on motivation.
